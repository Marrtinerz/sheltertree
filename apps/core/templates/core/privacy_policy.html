{% trans "1. Information We Collect" %}

{% trans "To provide our Service, we collect information in a few different ways." %}

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • {% trans "Account Information:" %} {% blocktrans %}When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, and a securely hashed password.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% trans "Profile Information:" %} {% blocktrans %}After signing up, you have the option to provide your first name, last name, user type (Renter or Homeowner), home country, and choose a "Shelter Sprite" avatar. This information helps personalize your experience.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% trans "Public Content:" %} {% blocktrans %}The core of ShelterTree is community content. We collect the information you publicly post, including property details, reviews (pros, cons, and ratings), questions, and answers.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% trans "Communications:" %} {% blocktrans %}When you contact us through our feedback form, sign up for a feature waitlist, or communicate with us in any other way, we collect the information you provide, such as your email address and/or WhatsApp number.{% endblocktrans %}

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • {% trans "Log & Usage Data:" %} {% blocktrans %}Like most websites, our servers automatically record information when you use ShelterTree, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and the pages you visit. We use this to secure and improve our service.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% trans "Cookies:" %} {% blocktrans %}We use cookies (small text files stored by your browser) to keep you logged in and to understand how you use our site. You can control these through your browser settings.{% endblocktrans %}

C. Information from Third Parties

  • {% trans "Google Sign-In:" %} {% blocktrans %} If you choose to sign up or log in using your Google account, we securely receive a limited set of information from Google, as permitted by your Google privacy settings. This includes:
    • Your primary email address (which we use as the unique identifier for your ShelterTree account).
    • Your first and last name (which we use to pre-fill your optional profile fields for your convenience).
    • Your profile picture URL (which we may use as your initial default avatar on ShelterTree).
    At no point do we ever receive or store your Google password. Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% trans "Property Data:" %} {% blocktrans %}When you add a new property, we use the Google Places API to help autofill details like the address and coordinates. We store the permanent Google Place ID as a reference.{% endblocktrans %}

  • {% trans "To Communicate With You:" %} {% blocktrans %}To send you important service updates, security alerts, and, with your consent, to notify you about new features or ask for your feedback. You can opt-out of non-essential communications at any time.{% endblocktrans %}
